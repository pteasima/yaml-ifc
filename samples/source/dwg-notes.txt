How it was opened
- Binary DWG, version AC1021 (AutoCAD 2007), 2,302,016 bytes. ezdxf cannot read DWG.
- No dwg2dxf, LibreCAD, or ODA converter was installed. LibreDWG 0.14.8601 was built under /tmp and used only to write a temporary DXF (/tmp/floorplan.dxf, 11 MB). That DXF was read with ezdxf 1.4.4.
- Decode warnings (not fatal): Invalid num_pages 0; Invalid SORTENTSTABLE.num_ents 89684; many unstable DIMASSOC / MATERIAL / TABLESTYLE objects. Text, layers, lines, and dimension measurements that were checked still look coherent.
- DXF code page: cp1250. All geometry is in model space (18,858 entities). Paperspace layouts Rozvržení1 (2 viewports) and Rozvržení2 (empty) do not hold the plan.

Units and extents
- $INSUNITS = 4 (millimetres). $MEASUREMENT = 1 (metric). $LUNITS = 2 (decimal). $DIMLFAC = 1, $DIMSCALE = 10.
- Header extents (mm): EXTMIN (4625798.257, -795669.465, -812.800), EXTMAX (5066998.002, -552128.151, 1371.600).
- Overall model size: 441.20 m × 243.54 m. This is the whole tiled model, not the house. It includes the site drawing (SITUACE), elevations (POHLEDY), sections (ŘEZ A-A … D-D), roof plan (PŮDORYS STŘECHY), ceiling plan (VÝKRES STROPU NAD 1.NP), and the sanitary plan (PŮDORYS 1.NP - ZTI), plus title blocks.
- Dimension measurements are in mm (samples: 200, 250, 1000, 1475, 2500, 3300, 4050, 4600).
- The architectural sheet titled PŮDORYS 1.NP sits around model x ≈ 4698–4744 m, y ≈ -615 to -599 m (coordinates are mm/1000). In that band, ignoring entities longer than 25 m:
  - layer silná: about 53.4 m × 16.0 m
  - layer šrafy: about 45.0 m × 16.1 m
  - layer zařízení (furniture): about 34.9 m × 15.0 m
  Room-name inserts for that copy span about 39.8 m × 12.0 m. So the 1.NP drawing is on the order of 35–50 m by 15–16 m, not the 441 m model extent. Only a 1.NP plan was found (no 2.NP title).

Walls: closed polylines or a line mess?
A line mess, not one closed polyline per room.
- silná: 68 closed LWPOLYLINE, 39 open, plus 142 LINE and 25 ARC. Closed ones are mixed sizes (many under 0.3 m, some 1–20 m). They are wall pieces, not room outlines.
- šrafy: 1677 closed LWPOLYLINE, mostly 4 vertices and 0.3–1 m across (wall-fill rectangles), plus 210 HATCH.
- tenka: mostly open LINE (3996), plus 164 closed / 96 open polylines.
- zařízení is its own layer, so furniture can be dropped without deleting walls.
- Almost no block inserts for furniture (78 INSERT total; 65 are dimension arrows "_Oblique"). Fittings are exploded geometry.

Verdict
Usable as the floorplan source. Units are millimetres, room names exist, walls and dimensions are on separate layers from furniture. It is not a clean set of closed room polygons: the plan has to be traced from silná/tenka/šrafy lines, and the model also contains site, elevations, sections, roof, and a duplicate ZTI plan.
